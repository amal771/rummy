How to play
  • Each player gets 13 cards. On your turn, draw one card (closed or open pile), then discard one.
  • To win, arrange all 13 cards into valid groups and declare with one card as your finishing discard.
  • You need at least two sequences, and one must be pure (no jokers). The rest can be sequences or sets.
  • The wild joker and printed jokers can stand in for any card, except in a pure sequence.
  • Losers score the value of their unmatched cards (A, K, Q, J, 10 = 10; max 80). Lowest total wins.
  • Dropping costs 20 before your first turn, 40 after. A wrong declaration costs 80.
